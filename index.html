VP Foundation School Itanagar– located at Richi Village, Jollang

Brought to Arunachal Pradesh by Smti. Taba Vinita Debia & Shri. Debia Padang., VP Foundation School in Richi Village, Jollang is dedicated to fostering holistic growth in a safe, hygienic, and mentally stimulating environment.

We combine time-tested Montessori principles with cutting-edge tech innovations like VR, Robotics, and VP Phonix. Our school ensures every child develops intellectual curiosity, emotional intelligence, physical coordination, and vibrant social skills.

Our Mission

To provide experiential and value-driven early childhood education that celebrates every child's individual style of learning.

Our Vision

To empower young learners with foundational life skills, creative thinking, and confidence to succeed.

Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the age criteria for Playgroup and Nursery admissions?

Children aged 2 to 3 years are eligible for Playgroup. For Nursery, the child should be between 3 to 4 years old as of the start of the academic session.

What are the school operating hours for VPFS Richi Village?

Our school functions from Monday to Saturday between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Classroom hours vary depending on the grade level.

How can parents track their child's daily activities?

Parents get access to the official VPFS 360° mobile app, which provides regular updates on attendance, activities, photos, notices, and direct teacher messaging.
